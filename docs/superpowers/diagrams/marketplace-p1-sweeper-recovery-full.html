The sweeper, 60 seconds at a time

Why a 60-second job exists at all, and where stock comes back from.

The hazard it removes

  • Checkout step 5 (Tx A) commits orders as PENDING
  • Step 8 (Tx C) is what flips them to PENDING_CONFIRMATION
  • A crash in between leaves reserved stock with no owner

How the loop stays safe

  • FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED — replicas never collide
  • Cancel writes a history row and an outbox row in one tx
  • catalog-svc consumes only OrderCancelled

The rest of the job

  • Deletes idempotency_keys older than 24 h
  • SWEEPER_INTERVAL=60s, PENDING_TIMEOUT=2m
  • Runs in the same binary as the order service
