The gateway, one request at a time

Everything a request passes through before a handler sees it.

Identity is the gateway's job

  • Incoming X-User-* is always deleted first
  • Only the gateway is reachable from outside
  • Services trust those headers because of that

The middleware chain, in order

  • RequestID → OTel span → access log → recover
  • CORS from CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS, credentials on
  • Token bucket per IP: auth routes 10/min, else 20/s burst 40
  • /healthz and /readyz sit outside it

JWKS and the role check

  • Fetched at startup, cached, refreshed every 5 min
  • Unknown kid triggers at most one refresh per 30 s
  • Another owner's product is 404, not 403
